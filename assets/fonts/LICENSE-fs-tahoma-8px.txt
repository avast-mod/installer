fs-tahoma-8px-bold.ttf ("fs Tahoma 8px Bold AVaSt") is an adaptation of fs Tahoma 8px made for
AVaSt by malidev: every pixel of every glyph is doubled to the right and each glyph is one pixel
wider, the way Tahoma Bold is drawn at small sizes. It was generated by make_bold.py in this folder
and is licensed under the same Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported license.
